# Learning Outcomes Contracts: Module 3: The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac™)

This document outlines the expected learning outcomes and interaction patterns for students completing each chapter within Module 3. These serve as "contracts" between the educational content and the learner, defining the skills and knowledge gained.

## Chapter 1: NVIDIA Isaac Sim - Learning Contract

**Title**: NVIDIA Isaac Sim: Photorealistic Simulation and Synthetic Data Generation

-   **Expected Outcome**: Upon successful completion of this chapter, the student will be able to:
    -   Explain the value of photorealistic simulation for training AI models in robotics.
    -   Describe how NVIDIA Isaac Sim facilitates synthetic data generation for perception tasks.
    -   Articulate the concepts of domain randomization and scene construction within Isaac Sim.
-   **Student Interaction**:
    -   Read and comprehend conceptual explanations of Isaac Sim's features.
    -   Analyze examples of synthetic data generated from Isaac Sim for training AI.
    -   Engage with self-assessment questions to test understanding of simulation benefits.

## Chapter 2: Isaac ROS — Hardware-Accelerated Perception and VSLAM - Learning Contract

**Title**: Isaac ROS — Hardware-Accelerated Perception and VSLAM

-   **Expected Outcome**: Upon successful completion of this chapter, the student will be able to:
    -   Explain the purpose and benefits of hardware-accelerated ROS 2 packages provided by Isaac ROS.
    -   Describe the fundamental principles of Visual Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (VSLAM).
    -   Understand how Isaac ROS components contribute to efficient perception and VSLAM in humanoid robots.
-   **Student Interaction**:
    -   Read and analyze explanations of Isaac ROS components and their acceleration.
    -   Examine conceptual diagrams of VSLAM pipelines.
    -   Understand the role of NVIDIA GPUs in accelerating perception tasks.

## Chapter 3: Nav2 — Navigation and Path Planning for Humanoids - Learning Contract

**Title**: Nav2 — Navigation and Path Planning for Humanoids

-   **Expected Outcome**: Upon successful completion of this chapter, the student will be able to:
    -   Identify the core components and functionalities of the Nav2 framework.
    -   Explain the unique challenges of path planning for bipedal humanoid movement compared to wheeled robots.
    -   Describe conceptual approaches for adapting Nav2's principles to humanoid navigation, considering balance and stability.
-   **Student Interaction**:
    -   Read and analyze explanations of Nav2's global and local planners.
    -   Examine conceptual diagrams of humanoid navigation strategies.
    -   Understand the interplay between perception, planning, and control for humanoid movement.
